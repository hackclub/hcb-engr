Popovers are now enabled for everyone! 🎉

Pages across HCB, like transactions and invoices, now open right on top of what you're looking at instead of taking you somewhere else. From there, you can upload receipts, leave comments, and hop around without losing your place.

When you're done, just close the popover and you'll be right back where you left off. 💫
