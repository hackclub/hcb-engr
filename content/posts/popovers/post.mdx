Popovers are now enabled for everyone! 🎉

Clicking a transaction now opens it right on top of your list instead of taking you to a separate page. From there, you can upload receipts, leave comments, and hop between transactions without losing your place.

When you're done, just close the popover and you'll be right back where you left off. 💫
